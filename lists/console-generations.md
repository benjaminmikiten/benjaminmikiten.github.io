I often forget which generation number each of the major consoles fall under and more importantly, which Sega consoles came out when and contemporary to which Nintendo Consoles
## **3rd Generation (1983–2003)**

- **Nintendo Entertainment System (NES)** (1983)
- **Sega Master System** (1985)
- **Atari 7800** (1986)

## **4th Generation (1987–2004)**

- **Sega Genesis (Mega Drive)** (1988)
- **Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES)** (1990)
- **TurboGrafx-16** (1987)
- **Neo Geo** (1990)

## **5th Generation (1993–2006)**

- **Sony PlayStation** (1994)
- **Nintendo 64** (1996)
- **Sega Saturn** (1994)
- **3DO** (1993)
- **Atari Jaguar** (1993)

## **6th Generation (1998–2013)**

- **Sony PlayStation 2** (2000)
- **Microsoft Xbox** (2001)
- **Nintendo GameCube** (2001)
- **Sega Dreamcast** (1998)

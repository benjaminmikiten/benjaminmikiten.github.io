## ROMs Resources
A collection of resources for obtaining ROMs. Questionably legal, you're of course only downloading ROMs for games you own already.

- https://r-roms.github.io/ 
- https://myrient.erista.me/

## Flash Carts
Cartridges that you can put an SD card into then load ROMs onto. 

### SD Card Support
- https://krikzz.com/our-products/cartridges/ -- the real deal
- https://www.aliexpress.us/w/wholesale-gameboy-flash-cart.html -- Deeply questionable
### Single Game
- https://funnyplaying.com/products/midnighttrace-gb-gbc-flash-cart

## Handheld Emulators
Linux or Android-based devices that will play ROMs.

- https://anbernic.com/
- https://www.goretroid.com/

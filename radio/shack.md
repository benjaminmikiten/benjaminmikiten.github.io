I got my amateur radio Technician license in September 2026. My first callsign was KJ5RTD, but I now have **W3WIZ**. I'm studying for my General license, and I'm a member of SADRC.

This page is the living inventory of my station. It's small right now, and I'll add to it as I build out a proper shack.

## Where I'm at

- **License:** Technician
- **Callsign:** W3WIZ (previously KJ5RTD)
- **Studying for:** General
- **Club:** SADRC

## The gear

- **Handheld:** Yaesu FT-5DR
- **Hotspot:** a WPSD hotspot at home
- **Antenna:** an N9TAX J-pole

## What I do with it

Most of my on-air time is digital. The WPSD hotspot lets me monitor the major YSF reflectors from the FT-5DR, so there's usually something to listen to in the evening.

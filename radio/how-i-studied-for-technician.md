I scored 100% on the Technician exam, and I'm proud of it. This is what I did, in the order I did it.

## 1. Videos first

I started with the HRCC (Ham Radio Crash Course) videos. They piqued my interest, and the explanations made me want to keep learning the theory underneath. They were great to put on in the background, and I remembered a fair number of the answers as moments from those videos.

## 2. Then the ARRL book

Once the vocabulary was familiar, I moved to the ARRL license manual.

It was extremely important to me to actually _learn_ this material and not just rote-memorize it. I'm interested in the topic, and I didn't want to speed-run to the end and realize I had to learn it all over again. I was haunted by the idea of passing with a 75%, getting on the air, and then getting one in four things wrong.

## 3. Then a ton of drilling

The last and biggest phase was drilling practice questions in the HamStudy app. Spaced repetition surfaced the questions I kept missing, and I kept going until I stopped missing them.

Once I could consistently score 100%, I scheduled my exam through HRCC's VECs, via their Discord.

## What I'd tell someone starting

- Don't open with the question pool cold. Get the concepts in your ears first.
- Learn it, don't just memorize it. You'll be the one on the air.
- Drill until you're consistently at 100%, then schedule the test.

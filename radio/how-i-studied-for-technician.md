I scored 100% on the Technician exam, and I'm proud of it. This is what I did, in the order I did it.

## 1. Videos first

I started with the HRCC (Ham Radio Crash Course) videos. Before I could answer a single question, I needed to hear the material explained in plain language, and a presenter talking through it was the easiest way in.

## 2. Then the ARRL book

Once the vocabulary was familiar, I moved to the ARRL license manual. The book is where the detail lives, and the videos made it much easier to read.

## 3. Then a ton of drilling

The last and biggest phase was drilling practice questions in the HamStudy app. Spaced repetition surfaced the questions I kept missing, and I kept going until I stopped missing them.

## What I'd tell someone starting

- Don't open with the question pool cold. Get the concepts in your ears first.
- Use the book for what the videos skip, not as your starting point.
- Drill a lot. This is where the score came from.

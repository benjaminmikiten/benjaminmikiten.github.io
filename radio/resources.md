The things I wish someone had laid out for me when I started. I'll keep adding to this.

## What I used for Technician

- **[HRCC videos](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=Ham+Radio+Crash+Course+technician)**: the Ham Radio Crash Course series. I started here.
- **ARRL license manual**: the [ARRL](https://www.arrl.org/) book for your license class. I read it after the videos.
- **[HamStudy.org](https://hamstudy.org/)**: practice questions with spaced repetition, also as an app. Most of my study time went here.

## Taking the exam

Exams are given by Volunteer Examiner Coordinators (VECs). I scheduled mine through HRCC's VECs, via their Discord, once I was consistently scoring 100% on practice questions.

## Reference

- **The question pool**: the exam draws from public pools published by the [NCVEC](https://www.ncvec.org/). Every question you drill is in there.
- **Part 97**: the FCC's amateur radio rules, in the [eCFR](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97).
